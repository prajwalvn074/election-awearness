What is an Election?

An election is a process through which eligible citizens choose their representatives by voting.

Why is Voting Important?

Voting allows eligible citizens to participate in the democratic process and choose their representatives.

📋 Election Process

1. Voter Registration

Eligible citizens register themselves as voters.

2. Election Campaign

Candidates communicate their ideas and policies to voters.

3. Voting

Eligible voters cast their votes according to the election rules.

4. Vote Counting

The votes are counted according to the applicable election rules.

5. Result

The official election result is declared.

✅ Important Voting Tips

  • Check reliable information about the election process.
  • Learn about candidates using reliable public information.
  • Follow official election rules and instructions.
  • Make your own informed choice.
  • Respect the choices of other voters.

💡 Election Awareness
